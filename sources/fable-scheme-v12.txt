**(2) New blocking problems**

1. **Step 6 now classifies every unread Charlotin row as unchecked** (`SCHEME.md:54-56`, `SCHEME.md:108-109`, `SCHEME.md:33`). Charlotin is a tracker. Rule 5 tells the coder to build bearer rows from its Outcome, Monetary Penalty and Professional Sanction fields. Step 6 says a consequence known only from a tracker, with the forum's own record not read, is `unchecked`. Coder A codes the Charlotin series from the fields and gets k and N in the hundreds. Coder B reads step 6 and puts every row whose decision was not opened into U, so the Charlotin headline collapses. Minimal fix, one sentence at Rule 5: state which it is. Either "Charlotin's fields are the record for Charlotin rows; step 6's tracker clause applies to AIID and court records only, and this is printed as a known bias", or "a Charlotin row is coded only after the linked decision is read; otherwise `unchecked`". The first is consistent with the special treatment already given at line 84.

2. **Step 6 sits last in a precedence list, so step 2 fires first on the same record** (`SCHEME.md:87`, `SCHEME.md:96`, `SCHEME.md:108`). Line 87 says resolution is set "by precedence". For Campact the claim is live per the law-firm summary, so step 2 gives `pending` before step 6 is reached. The seed row codes `unchecked`, which is the intended reading, but a coder following the numbering codes `pending`. P and U differ, and the same applies to an imposed-final consequence reported only in press, which step 1 would catch before step 6. Minimal fix: move the forum-record test to a gate before step 1, or add to line 87 "step 6 is tested first; steps 0 to 5 run only on claims and consequences read in the forum's own record".

3. **Ground `other` maps to no bucket** (`SCHEME.md:31`, `SCHEME.md:74-77`). The mapping covers building-the-model, operating-or-publishing, professional-duty and unstated. A claims entry coded `other` therefore makes no bucket reachable and no consequence answering it can enter any bucket. Coder A follows the list and the party stays `no-claim`. Coder B treats `other` like `unstated` and maps it to every chain role the party holds. M, N and T minus M differ for any event with such a claim. Minimal fix at line 77: one clause saying either "`other` maps like `unstated`, and is counted in the same printed number" or "`other` maps to no bucket; the count of such entries is printed beside T".

4. **Employer discipline has no forum record under step 6** (`SCHEME.md:90-91`, `SCHEME.md:108-109`). Step 0 makes the employer the forum. AIID learns of a firing or suspension from press quoting the employer. Coder A treats the quoted employer statement as the forum's own record and codes `imposed-final` in the operator bucket. Coder B treats the press report as press and codes `unchecked`. AIID k2 and U differ, and this is the most common operator consequence in AIID. Minimal fix at step 6: state whether the employer's own published statement, or a court or bar record noting the discipline, counts as the forum record, and that a press paraphrase alone does not.
