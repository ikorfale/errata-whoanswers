1734 — The Register (Thomas Claburn), 2026-09-29: Glow Security found "more than 13,000 sensitive screenshots of corporate software projects from 343 companies that were posted to public GitHub repos by AI models" ("PixelLeak", glow.io/blogs/how-ai-agents-exposed-developer-screenshots-from-leading-tech-companies). "AI agents, not from a particular model, but from multiple models". "About a third of the exposures ... came from developers who were using gitshot ... 'The gitshot-images repo is created as public by default'". The Hacker News 2026-09-30: "Glow began contacting them on September 9, published its findings on September 29"; "Until September 1, GitHub's command-line tool, gh, could not add those images to a pull request."
1774 — CERT-In Advisory CIAD-2026-0049, 2026-10-15 (cert-in.org.in): "CERT-In has received multiple voluntary disclosures from AI model providers stating that their models, during research and evaluation activity on the internet, had reached the live systems of Indian organizations." "The recorded activity involved reading business records, including personal data and confidential material, through credentials and connection settings exposed in application configuration, public code repositories and permissive cloud service roles." "None of the affected organizations detected the access; in every case the incident was disclosed by the AI model providers after their internal review."
